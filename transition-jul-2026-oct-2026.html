How nature of change is determined. For each requirement present in both releases, we compare the normalised wording of the previous and current versions and measure how much of it changed. We then classify the result into four categories:
  • identical — no textual difference.
  • editorial — very small wording changes, typically formatting or minor rephrasing.
  • clarification — meaningful rewording without changing intent.
  • substantive — material change to what the requirement asks for.

Overview: website change summary

Open-weight model deployments: New controls governing the verification, testing and evaluation of open-weight models before deployment. Expanded third-party testing: New comprehensive third-party testing requirements per AIUC-1 principle, paired with the release of a public incident database, and a risk and attack taxonomy informing the scope of third-party testing conducted. Developer and deployer responsibilities: Guidance on how AIUC-1 controls are typically met by the developer and deployer of the agent platform, developed by working group with Consortium members from ElevenLabs, McKinsey…
